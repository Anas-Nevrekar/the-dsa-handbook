152. Maximum_Product_Subarray
Difficulty: Medium
1. Problem Understanding
a. The goal is to find the largest product of a continuous sequence of numbers within a given array.
b. This sequence, called a subarray, must contain at least one number.
c. Numbers in the array can be positive, negative, or zero.

2. Approach
a. The key idea is that negative numbers can turn a very small (large negative) product into a very large positive product when multiplied.
b. Therefore, we need to track not only the maximum product ending at the current position but also the minimum product ending at the current position.
c. When iterating through the array, at each number, we consider three possibilities for the new maximum and minimum products: the number itself, the number multiplied by the previous maximum product, and the number multiplied by the previous minimum product.
d. Zeroes reset the product sequence, as any product involving zero becomes zero.

3. Algorithm Steps
a. Initialize `cur_max` and `cur_min` to 1. These variables will store the maximum and minimum product ending at the previous array position.
b. Initialize `ans` (the overall maximum product found so far) to `INT_MIN`.
c. First, iterate through the input array `nums` to find the largest single element. Update `ans` with this value. This ensures that cases where all products are negative or zero, and the maximum product is a single number, are handled correctly.
d. Begin a second iteration through the array `nums` using an index `i` from 0 to `nums.size()-1`. Let `n` be `nums[i]`.
e. If `n` is 0, it means any product sequence ending here is broken. Reset `cur_max` and `cur_min` back to 1 and continue to the next number.
f. Store the potential new maximum and minimum products: `temp1 = n * cur_max` and `temp2 = n * cur_min`. This step is crucial because `cur_max` will be updated first and that new value shouldn't affect the calculation for `n * cur_min`.
g. Update `cur_max`: it becomes the maximum of `n`, `temp1`, and `temp2`.
h. Update `cur_min`: it becomes the minimum of `n`, `temp1`, and `temp2`.
i. Update the overall `ans` by comparing it with the current `cur_max`.
j. After iterating through all numbers, return `ans`.

4. Time Complexity
O(N)

5. Space Complexity
O(1)
